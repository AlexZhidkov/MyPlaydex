## Question

What exact sport categories, skill level tiers (e.g., social, casual, competitive, open training), age groups, and cost tiers must be supported in the discovery filter taxonomy to cover Perth's most common casual sports without overwhelming users?

## Answer

Settled taxonomy and filtering facets for MVP:

1. **Sports Taxonomy (Individual First-Class Categories)**:
   - Soccer (Football)
   - Volleyball (Indoor & Beach)
   - Basketball
   - AFL (Australian Rules)
   - Tennis
   - Pickleball
   - Running
   - Social Fitness
   - Walking
   - Cricket
   - Other / Multisport
2. **Activity Formats & Intensity Tiers**:
   - Ruled out for MVP to keep categorization friction and UI clutter minimal.
3. **Cost Filter**:
   - Binary "Free Only" filter toggle in the UI.
   - Exact fee (e.g., "Free", "$5 gold coin", "$10 court hire") displayed on the Activity card as a descriptive label.
4. **Demographic Filters**:
   - Gender: Mixed, Women Only, Men Only.
   - Age: All Ages, Adults (18+), Juniors (<18), Masters (35+).
